Last updated: [DATE]

[PUBLISHER NAME] operates Empire Tap. This policy describes how the app handles information.

## Data the app stores

Empire Tap stores game progress, including virtual currency, businesses, taps, prestige progress, and market holdings locally on the device. This information is used only to restore the player's game progress.

## Advertising

If advertising is enabled in a released version, the app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads. Advertising choices and consent are managed as required by applicable law.

## Sharing

Game progress is not transmitted by the app. Advertising data is handled by the advertising provider when ads are enabled.

## Children

Empire Tap is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law. Do not enable personalized advertising for child-directed audiences.
